This Privacy Policy explains how **viup** ("the App", "we") handles information. viup is a personal software tool operated by a single individual (the "Operator") to publish original videos to the Operator's own social media accounts. It has no external users.

By authorizing viup to access an account, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Use of YouTube API Services

viup uses **YouTube API Services**. By using viup you are also bound by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

## 2. Information we access and collect

When the account owner signs in with Google or TikTok and grants permission, viup accesses only what is needed:

| Source | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google / YouTube | OAuth access and refresh tokens; the authorized channel's ID; statistics (views, likes, comments) of videos uploaded by viup | Upload videos to that channel; measure which videos perform well to improve future videos |
| TikTok | OAuth tokens; basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) | Send videos to that account's drafts inbox |

viup does **not** collect data about viewers, other channels or other users, and does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

## 3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate viup's features listed above. We do **not** sell, rent or share any information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Storage and security

- All data is stored locally on the Operator's own computer/server in a private database. Tokens are kept in files that are never published.
- Video statistics are refreshed periodically. Statistics are not kept longer than needed and stale copies are deleted or refreshed at least every **30 days**.

## 5. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke viup's access at any time:

- Google / YouTube: [Google security settings → Third-party apps with account access](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions)
- TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

After access is revoked, viup can no longer reach the account and all stored tokens and data obtained from that account are deleted within **7 days**. You can also request deletion at any time via the contact below.

## 6. Children

viup is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

## 8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at [github.com/Soetiee2207/viup/issues](https://github.com/Soetiee2207/viup/issues).

## Tóm tắt tiếng Việt

viup là công cụ cá nhân, chỉ truy cập tài khoản của chính người vận hành. Công cụ dùng **YouTube API Services** (xem [Chính sách quyền riêng tư của Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). Dữ liệu lấy về gồm token đăng nhập, ID kênh và số liệu video do viup đăng. Dữ liệu được lưu cục bộ, không bán và không chia sẻ cho bên thứ ba.

Bạn có thể thu hồi quyền ở [Cài đặt bảo mật Google](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions) hoặc trong cài đặt TikTok. Sau khi thu hồi, dữ liệu được xoá trong vòng 7 ngày.
